About this work

A yellow sun rises behind a mirrored peak, and thin yellow lines run out from the centre across the whole surface.

This is about the purest kind of motivation there is, the sort that comes from inside you and asks nobody for a reward. The lines leaving the centre are what happens next, because once you find that motivation in one part of your life it starts running into all the others.

That is why I think it is so profound. It does not stay where you found it. It charges everything else.
