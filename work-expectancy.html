About this work

In this series the green is being present. It is the layers of small things in a day that disappear if you stop paying attention. The brown is addiction, and it cuts into the green with hard edges that you cannot help noticing.

There is a knot in every piece in this series, and it stands for the Gordian knot, because seeing a problem clearly is not the same thing as solving it, and sometimes the only way out is to cut.
