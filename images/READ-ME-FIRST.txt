PUT YOUR PHOTOS IN THIS FOLDER
==============================

The site looks for these exact filenames (all lowercase, .jpg).
Rename your photos to match and drop them in here.

  hero.jpg                  Big image at the top. Best: Humble with you
                            beside it for scale, or Humble straight-on.
                            (ELEMENTALAUDIS2 or 3)

  humble.jpg                Innerysm: Humble, straight-on
  archetypes.jpg            Innerysm: Archetypes
  inner-strength.jpg        Innerysm: Inner Strength
  intrinsic-motivation.jpg  Innerysm: Intrinsic Motivation
  breath.jpg                Innerysm: Breath
  now-harmony.jpg           Now: Harmony (ELEMENTALAUDIS4, landscape)
  now-chaos.jpg             Now: Chaos (ELEMENTALAUDIS5)
  studio.jpg                Workshop / you working (the track-saw shot)
  portrait.jpg              You, in the studio
  social-share.jpg          What shows when someone shares the link.
                            Make it 1200 x 630 px, landscape.

Any file that is missing just shows a grey placeholder with its name on
it — the site will not break. Add photos as you get them.

SIZE THEM FIRST
---------------
Straight-off-the-camera photos are 5-10 MB and will make the site slow.
Aim for about 1600-2000 px on the long edge and under 500 KB each.

Free ways to do it:
  - squoosh.app (drag in, drag out, no signup)
  - On a Mac: Preview > Tools > Adjust Size
  - On Windows: Photos app > Resize

SHOOTING THE WORK
-----------------
Flat, even daylight. Camera square-on to the panel, not at an angle.
Plain wall, a margin of wall visible around the piece. No flash — it
kills the grain, which is the whole point.
