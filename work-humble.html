About this work

A corridor of light runs back into blue and violet, and it does not stop where the panel stops. The piece is about scale, and about the fact that there is always something bigger than whatever you happen to be standing in front of.

Near the bottom edge there is one small shape. That one is me. Simple next to all the rest of it, and still worth looking at, which is more or less how I understand my own place in the whole thing.

At 172.5 cm tall this is the largest piece in the Innerysm series, and it is the one travelling to Manchester in October.
