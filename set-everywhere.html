Now feat. Addiction series

Everywhere

A new work of twelve panels, hung together as one

Everywhere is twelve panels hung together as one. The green in every panel is the present moment, cut straight with a knife. The brown is addiction, and most of that walnut is not cut at all. I tear it apart by hand and inlay it as it is, because addiction does not ask for permission.

Everywhere takes its name from something I believe about addiction, that it can be found anywhere, and that is why the work is twelve panels rather than one.

Marquetry in walnut, chestnut and ash veneer on plywood, dyed by hand, framed, 2026. Each panel is a finished work and can be acquired on its own.
