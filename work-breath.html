About this work

Two curtains of figured eucalyptus draw back from a centre of burr, and that centre is the still point the whole panel breathes around.

Scattered across the surface there are small squares of darker veneer. Those are ideas, the kind that arrive out of nowhere while you are not chasing anything, because that is the only time they ever arrive.

Everything starts with a breath. Every change, every improvement, every idea in this panel. So the work asks very little of you, only that you stop, be here now, and breathe.
